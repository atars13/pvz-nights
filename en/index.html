Five Shifts at the Pickup Point

A horror game about the night shift at a parcel pickup point.

Support

Questions, bug reports or purchase issues: assistantnova8@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Restoring your purchase: open the game, tap “FULL VERSION” in the main menu and then “RESTORE PURCHASE”. Use the same Apple Account you bought the game with.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Data we collect

None. The game does not collect personal data, does not store it on any server and does not share it with third parties. There is no registration, advertising, analytics or third‑party tracking, and the game works offline.

Data on your device

Your progress (completed nights, unlocked endings and notes) and the player name, if you enter one, are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere. To delete this data, delete the app.

Notifications

At the end of the game you may be asked to allow notifications. If you allow them, the game shows one local notification created on your device as part of the story. No data is sent anywhere.

Purchases

The full version is purchased entirely through Apple’s App Store. We never receive or store your payment details. The game only asks the App Store whether the full version has been purchased.

Children

The game does not collect data from users of any age, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
